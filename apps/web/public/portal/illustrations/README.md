# Dashboard illustrations

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. These decorative PNG assets replace the dashboard card SVGs. Original generated files are preserved outside the project; these copies ship with the application.

## Final prompts

### payments

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: a raster illustration for a Fresh Phones customer portal action-card header.
Primary request: a polished, welcoming 2D image illustration.
Style/medium: contemporary flat editorial illustration, substantial filled color shapes, slightly organic silhouettes, subtle paper-grain texture, restrained fine dark outlines. Visibly illustrated and expressive. No photography, no photorealism, no 3D rendering, no wireframe or thin-line icon style.
Palette: coordinated lavender and violet with soft cyan, blush pink, warm cream, and small golden-yellow accents, suited to the existing purple portal.
Composition/framing: a single cohesive vignette on a wide landscape canvas, roughly 2:1 aspect ratio; centered, broad silhouette filling the central 80 percent, generous edge margins and clear readable forms at a small card size.
Backdrop: genuinely transparent alpha outside the illustrated objects. No solid rectangle, no checkerboard painted into the image.
Constraints: no readable writing, numbers, names, logos, watermark, or full user-interface screenshot. Use abstract short lines and shapes where a document has content.
Subject: an illustrated hand holding a violet smartphone with a simple installment-calendar grid on its screen, a small paper receipt beside it and a few golden coins. The scene communicates reviewing a payment schedule; relaxed, trustworthy, playful.
```
