# Landing-style dashboard illustrations, version 2

Status: prompts prepared. A built-in imagegen attempt on 2026-10-10 was rejected with `usage_limit_reached`; the provider reports a reset at Unix time `1791676517`. No version-2 images were produced, and the current dashboard images remain active. The CLI alternative requires explicit user selection and a configured `OPENAI_API_KEY`.

## Art direction

Match the public landing hero's sky-blue and blush palette, airy clouds, pearly bubbles and silver accents. Keep the figures and objects as refined 2D painted illustrations, drawing on the existing landing campaign's anime/cel-shaded style. Retain the portal's sky, teal and peach category palette.

## Style references

- `apps/web/public/video/hero/woman-floating-cloud-poster.webp` — atmosphere, palette and motifs.
- `apps/web/public/brand/campaign/fresh-start-v1.webp` — 2D illustration rendering.

### payments

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: a new raster illustration for a Fresh Phones customer portal action-card header.
Primary request: recreate the existing action-card imagery in the landing page's visual language.
Input images: reference 1 is the actual landing hero and controls its soft sky-blue/blush palette, cloud atmosphere, floating composition, pearly bubbles and reflective silver accents; reference 2 controls the polished 2D painted anime/cel-shaded rendering and contemporary young-adult illustration style. Do not copy the reference headline, logo, person, photograph, or complete composition.
Style: refined 2D digitally painted editorial illustration with elegant proportions, fine controlled outlines, softly layered cel shading, delicate material highlights and clear silhouettes at card size. Keep the illustration fully drawn, not a photograph. Match the landing page's airy dreamy feeling rather than the current chunky flat cartoon artwork.
Palette: sky blue, porcelain white, pale cyan, muted teal, silver, soft blush and warm natural skin tones. Navy for definition and small details. Very little violet; no dominant purple. Maintain generous light highlights.
Composition: cohesive wide 2:1 vignette, centered with important elements inside the middle 80 percent; airy margins, a softly illustrated white cloud supporting the objects, one or two small iridescent bubbles and tiny silver glints as restrained secondary accents.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha outside illustrated objects. No solid card, background rectangle or painted checkerboard.
Constraints: no readable text, numbers, brand logo, real personal data, watermark or full UI screenshot. Use abstract lines and shapes on all screens and documents. No real-life photos, photographic people, 3D rendering, SVG-style placeholder icons, oversized cartoon hands, dense floral foliage or dominant violet.
Subject: A naturally proportioned drawn hand with a navy sleeve holding a pale-silver smartphone with an abstract installment-calendar grid on its sky-blue screen. A small porcelain receipt and a few muted champagne coins float beside it above a soft white cloud. A subtle silver check badge and tiny iridescent bubble reinforce a calm, clear payment schedule.
```
